# Daxatej Global — Corporate Import & Export Website

An authentic, human-designed corporate website for **Daxatej Global**—an international trade and export firm headquartered in **Rajkot (PIN: 360410), Gujarat, India**.

---

## Strict Design & Architecture Standards Enforced

1. **Zero Emojis & Clean Typography**:
   - Zero unicode emojis and zero corrupted character encodings.
   - 100% custom, crisp SVG line icons.
   - Human-designed editorial typography featuring `Newsreader` (refined editorial serif for headings) and `Plus Jakarta Sans` (ultra-clean, highly legible UI and specifications font).

2. **No Purple or Blue Themes**:
   - Palette inspired by historic European & Indian commodity trading houses:
     - Warm Linen / Architectural Stone (`#FBFBFA`, `#F3F1EC`, `#FFFFFF`)
     - Deep Charcoal (`#141715`, `#1E2320`)
     - Heritage British Forest Green (`#1F3D2E`, `#EDF3EF`)
     - Raw Warm Bronze (`#8F5E28`, `#F8F2EA`)
     - Hairline tactile borders (`#E4E1D8`, `#2F3631`)

3. **Zero Font Overlapping / No Superimposition**:
   - All elements exist in strict, natural document flow, flexbox, or grid with dedicated line heights and generous spacing.
   - Badges, titles, cards, and images never overlap or clip.

4. **Zero AI Fluff / Authentic Commercial Trade Content**:
   - Real products:
     - **Sodium Starch Glycolate (SSG)**: USP/NF, BP, EP, IP Type A & B superdisintegrant.
     - **Croscarmellous Sodium**: Fast-acting tablet & capsule superdisintegrant.
     - **Ready Mix. Tablet Coating Polymers**: Pre-formulated film coating systems (Moisture Barrier, Enteric & Immediate Release).
     - **Methacrylic Polymers (L-100, L-100-55, L30 D)**: Precision enteric coating polymers and 100% aqueous dispersions.
     - **Colloidal Silicon Dioxide**: USP/NF, BP, EP, Food E551 glidant.
     - **Microcrystalline Cellulose (MCC)**: PH 101, PH 102, PH 112.
     - **Magnesium Stearate**: Vegetable Grade lubricant.
     - **Sortex Indian Spices**: Saurashtra Cumin Seeds (Jeera), Gondal Coriander Seeds (Dhania), High-Curcumin Turmeric, Dried Red Chilli (Teja/S17).
     - **Specialty Chemicals**: Containerized bulk process chemicals.
   - Real Gujarat maritime gateways: Mundra Port (APSEZ), Deendayal Port (Kandla), Pipavav Port.
   - IncoTerms 2020 matrix: FOB Mundra, CIF Destination, CFR, EXW Rajkot.
   - Dual RFQ engine: Instant formal email drafting (`daxxaglobal11@gmail.com`) and formatted WhatsApp dispatch.
   - Technical monograph modals with full physicochemical laboratory limits.

5. **100% Fully Responsive Across All Devices**:
   - **Smartphones (320px – 480px)**: Slide-down mobile drawer with dimmed backdrop and body scroll lock; horizontally swipeable filter chip bar; touch-friendly 44px+ tap targets; bottom-sheet specification modal; stacked action buttons.
   - **Tablets & Phablets (768px – 1024px)**: Fluid 2-column grids for pillars, specs, and logistics; horizontally scrolling data tables with preserved cell readability.
   - **Desktops & Laptops (1024px+)**: Expansive multi-column layouts with generous margins, crisp line heights, and zero horizontal overflow.
