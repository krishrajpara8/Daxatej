Sodium Starch Glycolate (Type A / B)

Sodium Starch Glycolate is the sodium salt of a cross-linked carboxymethyl ether of potato starch. Operating via rapid capillary fluid uptake and massive isometric swelling, it forces tablet matrices to disintegrate cleanly without gelatinous resistance.

Volumetric Expansion: Swells 200× to 300× its dry volume in aqueous media.
Process Versatility: Equally efficient in direct compression and wet granulation tableting.
Lubricant Stability: Retains high disintegration kinetics even with high magnesium stearate loadings.

Colloidal Silicon Dioxide (Colloidal Silica)

High-purity fumed sub-micron silica produced by continuous vapor-phase hydrolysis of silicon tetrachloride in an oxyhydrogen flame. Its branched aggregate chains and tremendous specific surface area drastically reduce internal powder friction.

Superior Glidant Action: Resolves powder bridging, capping, and weight variations in high-speed rotary presses.
Moisture Scavenging: Protects moisture-sensitive active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs).
Anti-Caking Efficiency: Keeps hygroscopic ingredients continuously free-flowing during storage.
